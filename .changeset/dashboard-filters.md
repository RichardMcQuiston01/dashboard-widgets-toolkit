Dashboard-level filters. Declare `filters` (`choice`, `number`, `boolean`, `text` and `dateRange`, with defaults, checked by `validateDashboardFilters`) and pass them with the chosen `filterValues` to `useWidgets` and `Dashboard`. Every provider receives the resolved values as `options.filters` (a date range as an ISO interval), cached payloads for different choices are kept apart (`filter.name=value` in the cache key), and changing a filter reloads only the widgets that use it, keeping their data on screen as `stale` meanwhile. A widget uses every filter unless its definition sets `filters: ['store']` (some) or `filters: []` (none); a loaded widget carries the values as `widget.filters`, and detail loaders receive them too. `Dashboard` shows a `FilterBar` above the cards when it has `filters` and `onFilterChange`; `useDashboardFilters` keeps the choices in state, and they are not saved in the layout. Core: `DashboardFilter`, `resolveFilterValues`, `filterValuesFor`, `usesFilter`, `filterCacheParts`, `validateDashboardFilters`, `filterValuesOf`, `loader.setFilterValues`, and `filters` / `filterValues` in the resolve options. React: `FilterBar`, `useDashboardFilters`, the `useDetailData` `filterValues` argument, the `filters` class slot, the `filters` and `filtersReset` labels, and `.dwt-filters` styles.
